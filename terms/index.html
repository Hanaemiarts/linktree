Terms of service

When ordering a commission from Hanaemi Arts, the following terms of service applies. Custom agreements can be made, so don't be shy to reach out. By paying a starting fee, the client agrees to the following terms.

  • The artworks are drawn digitally in 2480 x 3508 pixels at 300 ppi resolution unless otherwise agreed upon with the client.
  • The artworks will be delivered digitally, but the the buyer is permitted to print a physical copy. If a traditional commission is requested, additional shipping fees may apply.
  • Artworks follow Hanaemi's art style. If you are unsure what to expect, take a look at previous works to get a better understanding.
  • For each commission, the client will pay 50% upfront as a starting fee, and the rest upon completion. The precise amount of each piece will be estimated together with the client and presented before the customer confirms the order.
  • The artist reserves the right to post the artworks and the creation process on social media (eg. Instagram, Facebook and Twitch). If the commission is a surprise gift, the client can request that art is not posted until a later date.
  • A commission is a personal piece. The artist does not have the right to sell these specific art pieces to any other client, nor do the buyer have the right to resell them for profit.
  • Should the client cancel the commission once it is in progress, th the starting fee will not be refunded. However, no further payments need to be made.
  • The price includes two revisions per piece - once in the sketch phase, and once in the linework phase, if applicable. If the client wishes to have additional revisions made, these will be done for an additional fee depending on size. The client will recieve updates on the progress of the artworks.
  • If Hanaemi deems herself unable to fulfill the commission as agreed with the client, the Hanaemi reserves the right to cancel the commission. The client will then get a full refund.
